Privacy policy for Kids Shorts Uploader

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Kids Shorts Uploader is used only by the owner of the Chirpy Chick Learning YouTube channel to upload that channel's own videos.

What the app accesses

With the owner's permission, the app uses two Google permissions: uploading videos to the owner's YouTube channel (youtube.upload) and reading the status of those videos (youtube.readonly).

How data is stored

The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's own computer, encrypted with Windows data protection. Video titles, upload status and view counts are kept in a local database on that computer. Nothing is sent to any server other than Google's YouTube API.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared or used for advertising. The app does not collect any information from viewers, including children.

Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
