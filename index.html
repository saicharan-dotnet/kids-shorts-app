Kids Shorts Uploader

Kids Shorts Uploader is a private tool used by the Chirpy Chick Learning YouTube channel. It uploads short, original learning videos for young children to that one channel through the official YouTube Data API.

The tool is used only by the channel owner. It does not offer sign-up to the public and does not access anyone else's account.
